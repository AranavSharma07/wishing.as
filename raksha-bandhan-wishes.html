Raksha Bandhan wishes for a lifelong bond.

Raksha Bandhan is a chance to celebrate the memories, humour and care that make sibling relationships unique. Whether you are sending a message to your brother, sister or cousin, make it personal with a shared memory or a simple thank-you.

Create a rakhi card ↗

Choose the right words for the moment

Start with a message that matches your relationship and the way you normally speak. You can copy any example, edit it in your own words, and open the creator to add a recipient, sender name or photo. WishingASR creates the preview and downloadable image in your browser.

A MORE PERSONAL NOTE

Make the greeting sound like you

Before sending, add one detail the recipient will recognize: a shared memory, something you appreciate about them or a hope you genuinely have for the year ahead. Keep the wording natural. A simple, specific note often feels more thoughtful than a long collection of generic phrases.

  • Use a private shared memory to make a Rakhi wish feel different from a forwarded message.
  • If you are sending a greeting to a cousin or chosen sibling, personalize the wording to fit your relationship.
  • Check the recipient’s name and the Hindi script in the preview before sharing.
