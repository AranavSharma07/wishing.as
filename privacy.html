Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

WishingASR provides tools for creating personalized greetings. This page explains what to expect when using this static website.

Photos and greeting content

Photos selected for greeting cards are processed locally in your browser to create a preview and downloadable image. The website does not intentionally upload those photos to a WishingASR server. Do not choose a photo you do not have permission to use.

Information you enter

Names, sender details, and custom greeting text are used in your browser to personalize the preview and exported card. The current static version does not provide a server-side account or save greetings to a WishingASR database. If you use the contact form, your email application opens with the details you entered; a message is sent only if you choose to send it.

Local browser storage

Some optional preferences and saved wishes are stored in your own browser using local storage so they remain available on that device. The selected theme may be kept for the current browser session. These items are not synced to a WishingASR account or database. You can remove them by clearing this website's data in your browser settings; doing so may also remove saved wishes and display preferences.

Private messages and public pages

Names, sender details, photos, and custom message text entered in the greeting creator are used to build the preview and exported card in your browser. The site does not publish these personalized fields to a public gallery or automatically post them to social media. A message is shared only when you choose a share action or download and send the card yourself. Avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into any online tool. Public quote examples are generic sample greetings, not users’ private messages.

Advertising and cookies

This website includes the Google AdSense publisher script. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads and help prevent invalid activity, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. WishingASR does not control Google's advertising technology or the cookies it sets. Ad serving depends on approval, account settings, available inventory, and applicable policies.

Learn more in Google's advertising technology information. You can review ad personalisation options in Google My Ad Center and manage cookies in your browser settings. These settings do not necessarily disable all non-personalized advertising or other site storage.

External links

Links to third-party services are governed by those services' own privacy practices. WishingASR is not responsible for their content or policies.
