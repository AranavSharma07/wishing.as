Deepotsav provides tools for creating personalized greetings. This page explains what to expect when using this static website.

Photos and greeting content

Photos selected for greeting cards are processed locally in your browser to create a preview and downloadable image. The website does not intentionally upload those photos to a Deepotsav server. Do not choose a photo you do not have permission to use.

Information you enter

Names and custom greeting text are used in your browser to personalize the card. The current static version does not provide a server-side account or save your greetings to a Deepotsav database. Your browser or device may keep temporary site data separately.
