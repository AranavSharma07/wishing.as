A RESPECTFUL FESTIVAL GREETING

Chhath Puja wishes filled with faith and gratitude.

Chhath Puja is observed with devotion, discipline and gratitude to the Sun and nature. A respectful greeting can share good wishes for health, peace and family well-being. Dates and local observance details can vary, so check a trusted regional calendar for the current year.

GUIDE 01

Traditional and respectful wishes

Keep the message simple, sincere and mindful of the meaning the festival holds for the recipient.

MAKE IT PERSONAL

Small details make a message memorable

Use the recipient’s name, mention a shared memory or recognize something you appreciate about them. Keep the message comfortable for the relationship and edit any example before sending.

  • Prefer specific words over long strings of generic adjectives.
  • Check the tone before sending a message in a workplace or family group.
  • Ask before posting a photo or personal detail about someone else.
