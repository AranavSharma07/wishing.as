ABOUT AA GROUP

A clear home for

AA Group provides the company identity and direction for its products. Its current public product portfolio includes WishingASR, a browser-based platform for personalised greetings, creative tools and occasion-based messages.

Our approach is grounded in clarity, usability, accessibility and thoughtful presentation. We aim to make digital experiences feel welcoming across phones, tablets and desktop screens.
