A LITTLE ABOUT US

Words that make
moments matter.

WishingASR is a browser-based greeting-card creator for festivals, milestones, and everyday affection. We aim to make it simple to choose an occasion, personalise a message in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and create a card to download or share.

Photos used in the greeting-card creator are handled locally in your browser for the card preview and export. WishingASR does not provide user accounts or a server-side greeting library in this static version.
