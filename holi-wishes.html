A MORE PERSONAL NOTE

Make the greeting sound like you

Before sending, add one detail the recipient will recognize: a shared memory, something you appreciate about them or a hope you genuinely have for the year ahead. Keep the wording natural. A simple, specific note often feels more thoughtful than a long collection of generic phrases.

  • Use “safe and joyful Holi” when sharing with a broad audience.
  • Avoid sharing photos of people covered in colour without their permission.
  • For professional contacts, choose a simple wish rather than slang or inside jokes.
